# Beginner Hiragana Recording Script

## Recording notes

- Record every isolated kana listed for a row.
- Record every example word as a complete word, not as separately joined kana.
- Use a neutral, clear teaching voice at a natural but unhurried pace.
- Leave about 300 ms of silence before and after each item.
- Say only the Japanese item. Do not include its romaji or English meaning.
- Separate files are ideal. A single row file also works if each item has a two-second gap.
- The English meanings below disambiguate words that can have multiple readings or pitch patterns.

## Special notes

- Record を by itself as `o`, its normal modern pronunciation. It does not need a standalone vocabulary example because it is primarily a grammatical particle.
- Record は by itself as `ha`. The `wa` particle pronunciation can be recorded later as part of a complete sentence.
- Record へ by itself as `he`. The `e` particle pronunciation can also wait for sentence audio.
- Keep じ and ぢ as separate files even though they normally share the same pronunciation. Do the same for ず and づ.
- The words かき, さけ, はな, and くも have other meanings with different pitch accents. Use the supplied English meaning when choosing the intended pronunciation.
